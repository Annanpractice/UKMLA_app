Native Android offline reading companion. The existing UKMLA app, Luna, question generation and quality checkpoints are unchanged.

**Install:** download `UKMLA-Offline-Reader-v0.1.0-arm64.apk` below on your S24 Ultra and allow installation from your browser/file manager. Android 9+ ARM64. This preview uses a development signing key; do not use it for sensitive personal information.

**Enable AI once:** open Model & info → Download recommended GGUF (Qwen3 4B Q4_K_M, approximately 2.5 GB) → Import GGUF from device. Keep about 5 GB free for import. After import, turn on airplane mode: cards, glossary, retrieval, explanations and follow-up chat all work locally. The APK has no INTERNET permission. Downloading the model initially uses your browser.

**Use:** Search a short term, or open a card and press/hold to select text → Explain / Summarise. Android text selection in other apps may also offer this reader via the system Process Text menu. No local match offers an optional, explicitly unverified possible meaning. Follow-ups remain in the current reading session and are not saved.

Includes 983 existing UKMLA cards and 1,258 NCI glossary entries selected because their terms appear in the cards. The glossary uses US wording and is not comprehensive. NCI attribution and source links are retained. AI explanations are not validated revision content and should be checked against the source cards.

The build checks compilation, prompt handling, database integrity, APK signing and absence of internet permission. S24 Ultra speed, memory, thermal behaviour and medical answer quality still require on-device evaluation. This is a first preview, not a clinically validated assistant.
